How Often to Water a Bromeliad

Guzmania spp. · Difficulty: Easy

Flowering 🐾 Non-toxic to cats & dogs
☀️ Summer / active growth Every 7-9 days (plus keep the central cup topped up)
❄️ Winter / dormant Every 12-16 days

Signs of Overwatering

A rotting, mushy base and stagnant, murky water sitting in the central cup for too long point to overwatering.

Signs of Underwatering

Dry, curling leaf tips and an empty central cup mean the plant needs both a soil watering and a top-up of its central reservoir.

Adjusting for the Seasons

Unlike most houseplants, bromeliads absorb much of their water through the central cup formed by their leaves, not just the roots - flush and refill that cup regularly.

Pro Tip

Empty and refresh the water sitting in the central cup every couple of weeks to prevent stagnant water and rot, rather than just topping it up indefinitely.
