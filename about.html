About Leafwise

Leafwise started as a simple question: why is it so hard to find a straight answer to "how often should I water this?" Most advice online is vague ("water when dry") or contradictory. We wanted plain, specific guidance you can actually use, backed by a free calculator that adjusts for your real conditions.

We are not botanists - we're plant owners who got tired of killing pothos plants. The guidance here reflects widely-used, general care practices for common houseplants. Always adjust based on what you observe in your own home, since light, humidity, and pot material vary a lot from one household to another.
