How Often to Water a Monstera Deliciosa

Monstera deliciosa · Difficulty: Easy

☀️ Summer / active growth Every 7-10 days
❄️ Winter / dormant Every 14-21 days

Care Snapshot

LightBright indirect light
SoilChunky, well-draining aroid mix (bark, perlite, potting soil)
HumidityPrefers 40-60% humidity for best leaf splits
Temperature18-27°C (65-80°F)

Signs of Overwatering

Yellow lower leaves combined with wet, heavy soil days after watering point to overwatering. The aerial roots can also start to rot at the base if the pot never gets a chance to dry.

Signs of Underwatering

Leaves droop and curl inward along the veins, and new growth stalls. Monstera is fairly vocal about being thirsty, which makes it easier to catch than plants that hide stress.

Adjusting for the Seasons

A Monstera actively pushing out new fenestrated leaves in spring/summer drinks noticeably more than the same plant sitting dormant in a cold winter room.

Pro Tip

Use a moisture meter or a wooden skewer - if it comes out with damp soil clinging to it past the middle of the pot, hold off watering.
