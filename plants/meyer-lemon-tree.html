How Often to Water a Meyer Lemon Tree

Citrus x meyeri · Difficulty: Hard

Novelty & Specialty ☠️ Toxic to cats & dogs (citrus oils and psoralens - causes vomiting, drooling and sensitivity to light)
☀️ Summer / active growth Every 3-5 days
❄️ Winter / dormant Every 7-9 days

Care Snapshot

LightVery bright light, ideally several hours of direct sun or a full-spectrum grow light
SoilWell-draining citrus or cactus potting mix
HumidityAverage home humidity is fine (30-50%)
Temperature18-27°C (65-80°F); avoid cold drafts and sudden temperature swings

Signs of Overwatering

Yellowing leaves that drop across the whole canopy, not just the bottom, along with soil that stays wet for many days, point to root rot.

Signs of Underwatering

Leaves curl and drop, and any developing fruit can shrivel or fall prematurely if the soil dries out for too long during fruit set.

Adjusting for the Seasons

Indoor citrus needs the brightest spot in the house or, realistically, a grow light through the darker months - insufficient winter light is the most common reason indoor lemon trees fail to fruit.

Pro Tip

Hand-pollinate the fragrant white flowers indoors with a small paintbrush, dabbing pollen from flower to flower - without bees or wind indoors, this is usually necessary to get any fruit to actually form.
