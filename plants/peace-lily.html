How Often to Water a Peace Lily

Spathiphyllum · Difficulty: Easy

☀️ Summer / active growth Every 5-7 days
❄️ Winter / dormant Every 10-14 days

Care Snapshot

LightLow to medium indirect light
SoilRich, moisture-retentive potting mix with good drainage
HumidityLikes higher humidity (50%+) but adapts
Temperature18-27°C (65-80°F)

Signs of Overwatering

Yellow leaves and a mushy, blackened stem base signal overwatering - peace lilies are one of the few plants where root rot progresses quickly once it starts.

Signs of Underwatering

This is the one houseplant that dramatically dramatically droops the entire plant flat when thirsty, then perks back up within a couple of hours of watering - a very clear, low-risk warning signal.

Adjusting for the Seasons

Because peace lilies like consistently moist (not soggy) soil, the interval changes less between seasons than for succulent-type plants, but you can still stretch it out in winter when the furnace dries out the room less often.

Pro Tip

Let the dramatic droop be your built-in reminder - just don't make a habit of waiting for it every time, as repeated wilting stresses the roots.
