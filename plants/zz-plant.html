How Often to Water a ZZ Plant

Zamioculcas zamiifolia · Difficulty: Very easy

☀️ Summer / active growth Every 14-18 days
❄️ Winter / dormant Every 25-35 days

Signs of Overwatering

Yellow, swollen-looking stalks and leaves that drop off in clumps are classic overwatering symptoms. ZZ plants store a large reserve of water in underground rhizomes, so they almost never need water as often as people assume.

Signs of Underwatering

It takes prolonged, severe drought before a ZZ plant shows real stress - leaves may shrink slightly and lose their glossy shine, but this plant is built to survive long dry spells.

Adjusting for the Seasons

In low-light winter conditions many ZZ plants can go over a month between waterings; always check the rhizomes are firm, not squishy, if you're ever unsure.

Pro Tip

Treat the ZZ plant more like a succulent than a tropical foliage plant when deciding how often to water.
