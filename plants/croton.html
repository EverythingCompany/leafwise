How Often to Water a Croton

Codiaeum variegatum · Difficulty: Moderate

☀️ Summer / active growth Every 5-7 days
❄️ Winter / dormant Every 10-14 days

Care Snapshot

LightBright light, some direct sun for best color
SoilWell-draining, rich potting mix
HumidityPrefers higher humidity (40%+)
Temperature18-27°C (65-80°F), dislikes drafts and sudden temperature swings

Signs of Overwatering

Yellow leaves dropping in clusters and a mushy stem base indicate overwatering and root rot.

Signs of Underwatering

Dry, crispy leaf edges, dulling leaf color, and leaf drop show up when a croton has gone too long without water.

Adjusting for the Seasons

Croton reacts dramatically to any change - sudden leaf drop after a move or a cold draft isn't always a watering problem, so rule those out first.

Pro Tip

Consistency matters more than the exact interval - crotons dislike swinging between soggy and bone dry, so aim for evenly moist soil.
