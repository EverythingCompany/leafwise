How Often to Water a Snake Plant

Dracaena trifasciata · Difficulty: Very easy

☀️ Summer / active growth Every 14-21 days
❄️ Winter / dormant Every 28-42 days

Signs of Overwatering

Mushy, translucent leaf bases and a rotten smell near the soil line are the telltale signs of overwatering. Snake plants store water in their thick leaves, so soggy soil is by far the most common way people kill this plant.

Signs of Underwatering

Underwatering shows up as thin, slightly curled leaves, but a snake plant can go many weeks without water and still look fine - it is far more tolerant of drought than of soggy roots.

Adjusting for the Seasons

In winter, when growth almost stops, many snake plants only need watering once a month or even less. Always let the soil dry out completely between waterings, in every season.

Pro Tip

When in doubt, wait another week. Nearly every snake plant problem traces back to watering too often rather than too little.
